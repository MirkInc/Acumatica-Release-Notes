# Acumatica ~ MIRK.Customizations Release Notes

Comparison: `2026.05.01a to 2026.06.12`  
Build: `25.201.0213`

## Features Overview

The 2026.06.12 package updates MIRK.Customizations from 2026.05.01a to 2026.06.12.

- Adds salesperson commission processing, including a new commission screen with period selection, summary totals, detailed commission rows, generation, and voiding.
- Adds support for salesperson guarantees and new-business incentive tracking so commission calculations can include regular commissions, guarantee adjustments, and new-business amounts.
- Adds commission exclusion setup so selected accounts can be left out of commission calculations.
- Improves MIRK cash conversion handling for AR and AP payments, including late applications, voided payments, payment charges, and failed conversion retries.
- Adds equipment registration and purchase-tracking improvements so internal equipment records can retain registration status, keep-registered reasons, PO references, bill references, payment references, and capitalized cost information.
- Improves tax-exempt sales order behavior by keeping line tax zones synchronized to the customer location tax zone when tax-exempt logic applies.
- Refines DocuSign auto-place field handling and e-sign setup defaults carried forward from the previous package.

## Features Removed or Altered

No user-facing package files were removed.

The following behavior was altered:

- Commission processing is now controlled by the MIRK setup option `Enable Salesperson Commission Processing`.
- Salesperson and customer screens now expose new-business incentive fields when commission processing is enabled.
- Cash conversion now tracks AP adjustment conversion status in addition to existing AR adjustment and AR payment-charge conversion status.
- Equipment purchase fields are locked or shown based on whether the equipment was created through PO/AP activity and whether purchase data is already linked.
- Equipment registration fields now require a reason when the equipment should stay registered.
- DocuSign auto-place setup now updates existing records by anchor prefix, allowing corrected tab-type metadata to be applied to existing defaults.

#### Code-Level Changes

- Adds `MIRKCommissionProcessing`, a new processing graph for generating and voiding commission rows by financial period.
- Adds commission detail, summary, period, type, guarantee, and excluded-account DACs.
- Adds a setup-level commission processing flag and shared helper methods so commission UI is enabled only when configured.
- Adds salesperson guarantee records and new-business incentive fields on salesperson/customer-related extensions.
- Adds generated commission summaries for regular commission, prorated guarantee, guarantee adjustment, new-business commission, and total commission.
- Adds safeguards so commission generation cannot skip required periods and voiding clears generated detail/summary rows for the selected period.
- Adds account exclusions to commission processing and setup maintenance.
- Adds late-application cash conversion logic for AR adjustments, AR payment charges, and AP adjustments.
- Adds AP adjustment `UsrMIRKCashConverted` tracking and resets conversion status when payment application rows are inserted or changed.
- Repairs AR voided unapplied balance values for voided payment scenarios.
- Updates cash conversion retry handling to use the payment cash account when prior vendor conversion attempts failed.
- Adds equipment registration fields, registered-state list values, and keep-registered reason validation.
- Adds purchase linkage fields for equipment PO, bill, payment, payment method, invoice amount/date, purchase paid status, and capitalized cost.
- Adds equipment purchase/registration data carry-forward in customization setup.
- Keeps tax-exempt SO line tax zones synchronized when the customer location is tax exempt.
- Updates DocuSign auto-place setup to match defaults by anchor prefix and keep conditional PI fields aligned.
