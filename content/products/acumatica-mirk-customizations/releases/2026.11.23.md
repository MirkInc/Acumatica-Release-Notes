# Acumatica ~ MIRK.Customizations Release Notes

Comparison: `2026.08.04 to 2026.11.23-002`

Build: `25.201.0213`

## Features Overview

This release expands equipment maintenance, rental components, branch labor calendars, and work-order billing. It also improves vendor corrections, commission calculations, and customer information. Administrators should use **Migration Settings and Post-Publish Setup** below: publishing the customization does not configure every feature, and this ZIP has deployment prerequisites that must be addressed first.

### Equipment Maintenance and Service History

- Adds maintenance matrices for internal and external equipment so service teams can review maintenance status across multiple units and open the related history.
- Supports maintenance scheduled by time, mileage, or whichever threshold is reached first. A Logging Only option records maintenance and a meter reading without creating a due threshold.
- Shows when the required equipment meter has not been configured, instead of treating a missing reading as usable maintenance information.
- Adds manual maintenance entry and records the completion meter reading and meter type. Work-order maintenance records retain their source reference and associated attachments.
- Lets stock items be excluded from maintenance tracking when they should not participate.

### Equipment Meters and Rental Tickets

- Adds a combined meter-history view for internal equipment, with primary, secondary, and tertiary readings, source-document links, and access to individual log details.
- Carries secondary and tertiary starting readings through rental records and rental, return, transfer, and exchange tickets.
- Expands ticket screens to show the additional meters, including the exchanged unit's starting readings.
- Corrects internal equipment's secondary-meter logging so new readings go to the internal equipment history.

### Assigned Rental Components

- Lets rental teams assign component equipment to a parent unit and choose whether each component is free or billed.
- Adds the assigned components to a rental agreement when the parent equipment is selected, with an Apply Components action for eligible existing lines.
- Keeps free components at zero price through invoicing and tracks the relationship between the parent and its component lines.
- Protects issued equipment from component replacement and asks users to confirm changes to an existing component group.

### Branch Labor Calendars

- Adds three separately configurable branch labor calendars alongside the existing general and employee calendars.
- Lets dispatch teams choose the branch for each additional calendar in MIRK/PLREI Setup.
- Corrects all five labor calendars' screen bindings to reference the current work-order components.

### Work-Order Segments, Add-On Charges, and Release

- Supports MIRK work-order workflows within Rental360's segment workflow, including employee assignment copying, eligible segment invoicing, and parent closure after the related work is complete.
- Adds work-order-type defaults for add-on charges and carries those charges into work orders and invoices.
- Coordinates invoice release with the source work orders, maintenance posting, and parent closure. Repeated release attempts are tracked to avoid repeating completed work.
- Prevents scheduling-board edits to canceled work and work awaiting billing, already invoiced, or pending closure.

### Accounting and Customer Tools

- Strengthens Change Vendor handling for eligible AP documents, including validation of the replacement, reversal, and settlement. The dialog now includes payment-method and cash-account selections when settlement requires them.
- Adds Change PO Nbr on sales orders and rental agreements, and View Customer from Customer Details.
- Shows a preview of the recipients for a customer's selected active mailing, including missing-email indicators and duplicate suppression.
- Adds an optional Auto Mail Payment Receipt preference. **This release stores the preference only; it does not send payment receipts or schedule email automation.**
- Improves equipment purchase summaries by considering discounts, document release, and partial or line-level payment status.
- Corrects payment-charge cash conversion to use the configured cash account's branch and subaccount, including clearing-account mappings.

### Salesperson Commissions

- Calculates the guaranteed-commission shortfall after both regular commissions and new-business incentives, so both earnings contribute toward the guarantee.
- Retains eligible activity without an assigned salesperson in a zero-rate, unassigned group instead of omitting it from the generated results.
- Separates salesperson guarantee and new-business incentive settings into dedicated tabs.

## Features Removed or Altered

### Maintenance and Meter History

Maintenance types now validate the settings required by their schedule basis. Time-based types need a positive time interval; mileage-based types need a positive mileage interval and a matching meter type. Logging Only types also require a meter type.

Internal secondary-meter history now reads the internal log table. Historical internal readings previously written to the external table are **not automatically moved** and may no longer appear in the internal history view. Reconcile affected history separately using verified equipment ownership and source documents.

### Rental Component Changes

Managed components use the Free/Bill selection to control pricing. Direct changes to their free-item flag are restricted, and equipment with rental activity cannot be freely replaced or removed. Imports and API calls cannot answer the interactive parent/component replacement confirmation; remove eligible component lines before changing the parent through those channels.

### Work-Order Completion and Invoicing

Finishing service work, creating an invoice, and releasing/closing a work order are distinct steps. Billable MIRK work must be completed, off hold, and in Needs Billed before invoicing. Nonbillable MIRK work uses Pending Closure for release. Parent orders remain open until their child segments meet the closure rules and any billable parent work has a released invoice.

Grouped invoices require valid source-work-order references on their lines. The release process rejects missing or inconsistent references rather than guessing which work to close. The scheduler treats finished service work as locked.

### Commission Results

New-business earnings now reduce the remaining guarantee payment. Previously generated periods are not automatically recalculated by publishing this package; any intended correction must use the commission process's existing void/regenerate workflow.

### Packaged Screens and Inquiry Content

The separate NAWMIRK screen extensions for Work Order Types and Work Orders are removed from this ZIP; the MIRK extensions now include the add-on controls. Coordinate with any separately published package that still supplies those controls to avoid duplicate definitions.

The `MIRK-MyWorkOrders` generic inquiry definition is no longer bundled, although the My Work Orders navigation entry remains. This omission does not prove that an existing tenant's inquiry will be deleted. Preserve the inquiry on upgrades, and supply it separately for a new tenant if that navigation entry is needed.

### Migration Settings and Post-Publish Setup

Use this checklist when upgrading from `2026.08.04`. **Complete the deployment prerequisites before making the new DLL available to users**, then configure the applicable features after publication. A database field or screen file being included does not mean that its tenant-specific settings or navigation have been populated.

#### Deployment Prerequisites

1. **Use a compatible Rental360 package.** The new DLL directly references Rental360 segment, scheduler, ticket, and work-order release APIs. The bundled scheduler migration identifies Rental360 `260930` as its prerequisite; the exact compatible Rental360 binary must also expose the referenced APIs. The unchanged Acumatica build label alone does not establish Rental360 compatibility.
2. **Publish the included customer preference schema.** The manifest declares `Customer.UsrMIRKAutoMailPaymentReceipt` as a nullable Boolean. A separate column-creation script is no longer required. The column declaration contains no database default or bulk null-to-false update; the DLL defaults new preferences to false and normalizes a null preference to false when a customer is saved through its maintenance graph. The preference remains optional and does not send receipts.
3. **Publish the maintenance schema and bundled repair together.** Revision `-002` retains the `MIRKMaintenanceLog` table, all columns, primary key, and NoteID index. The unfiltered work-order-line index declaration is removed; `MIRKMaintenanceLog_IndexRepair_20261123` creates or replaces that index with the intended non-null source-key filter. No manual index-declaration removal is required for this package. The table and `WorkOrderLineNbr` column must exist when the repair executes.
4. **Provide the scheduler script's dependencies.** The included `MIRKSegmentSchedulerStatuses` script requires `NVRTCalendarStatusColor`, `NVRTWorkOrder`, and `MIRKSetup`. Deploy the compatible Rental360 calendar schema before running it. No separate scheduler seed script needs to be added to this package.
5. **Provide navigation and access for the new screens.** Frontend files for `MIRK2013`, `MIRK2014`, and `MIRK9002`–`MIRK9004` are included, but corresponding new site-map nodes are not present in this manifest. If absent in the target tenant or companion customization, register the screens in the site map/workspace and assign access rights before handing them to users.

#### Bundled Database Scripts

Both scripts are embedded in `project.xml`; they do not appear as standalone ZIP files. Their attributes select native SQL Server/Azure execution and skip MySQL.

- `MIRKMaintenanceLog_IndexRepair_20261123` replaces an existing index or creates a missing one on `(CompanyID, WorkOrderType, WorkOrderNbr, WorkOrderLineNbr)`. Uniqueness applies only where the work-order type, number, and line number are all non-null. Historical/manual rows with incomplete source references are excluded. The script changes no maintenance records, and its transaction rolls back if index creation fails, including when fully populated source keys are duplicated.
- `MIRKSegmentSchedulerStatuses` inserts missing status/color rows for Needs Billed, Backlog, In Review, Invoiced, Cancelled, Pending Closure, Pending Customer Approval, Accepted, and Open. It targets company `0` plus company IDs present in `MIRKSetup`, `NVRTWorkOrder`, or existing calendar colors at execution time. New rows are visible; existing labels, colors, and visibility remain unchanged. The script does not enable segments or configure tenants created later.

#### Automatic Publication Behavior and Historical Data

- The customization plugin initializes null MIRK and NAW work-order-type line counters and inserts missing default DocuSign auto-place anchors. Existing anchors are preserved. DocuSign defaults are not a new feature in this comparison; they remain part of publication setup.
- The two embedded database scripts provide index repair and scheduler defaults as part of publication. They are separate from the customization plugin; source-project `Sql/` files are not automatically executed just because they exist on disk.
- Publication does **not** assign calendar branches, enable optional feature switches, populate component assignments, create add-on definitions, bulk-update customer preferences, migrate historical meter readings, or replay historical work-order releases.
- The new release-state table is for tracking coordinated releases. Do not bulk-release completed historical work merely to populate it.
- The bundled filtered index excludes historical maintenance logs with incomplete work-order source references; publication does not delete those records.
- Both `BAccount.UsrMIRKNewBusinessCancelled` and `BAccount.UsrMIRKNewBusinessCanceled` remain in the current manifest. The DLL uses the single-L **Canceled** field, as the previous DLL already did. The newly included column declaration is not an automatic data copy between the two spellings.
